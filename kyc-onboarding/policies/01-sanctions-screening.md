# Policy 01 — Sanctions & Watchlist Screening

Every applicant must be screened against the firm's sanctions and watchlist
sources before onboarding. A positive or partial watchlist match is an
automatic trigger for **enhanced due diligence** and may not be fast-tracked to
`clear`. Where the screening provider is unavailable or returns an ambiguous
result, the application must be routed to a human compliance officer rather than
auto-cleared. Screening must cover the applicant only; screening third parties
on an applicant's behalf is prohibited.
