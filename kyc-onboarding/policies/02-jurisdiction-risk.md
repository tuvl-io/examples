# Policy 02 — Jurisdiction Risk Ratings

Applicant risk is influenced by the country of residence. The firm maintains a
tiered rating:

- **Standard** (`us`, `gb`, `de`): baseline due diligence. Eligible for
  fast-track `low` banding when screening is clean and no other flag applies.
- **Elevated** (`sg`): standard checks plus a documented source-of-funds note;
  band no lower than `medium` unless a compliance officer approves.
- **Enhanced** (`in`): enhanced due diligence required; a human compliance
  review is mandatory before approval regardless of screening outcome.

A clean sanctions screen does not by itself override a jurisdiction's enhanced
rating.
