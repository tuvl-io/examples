# Travel & Expense Policy

This policy governs reimbursable business expenses.

- **Meals while travelling:** the daily meal allowance is **$75 per day**,
  covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- **Ground transport:** economy rideshare and public transit are reimbursable;
  premium/luxury tiers are not.
- **Hotels:** book through the corporate portal; the nightly cap is **$250**
  before taxes.

Submit expenses within **30 days** of the transaction date. Receipts are
required for any single expense over **$25**.
