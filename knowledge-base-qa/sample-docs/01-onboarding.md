# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company. This guide covers your first week.

- **Day 1:** Collect your laptop and access badge from the IT desk on the 3rd floor.
- **Day 2:** Complete the mandatory security-awareness training in the LMS.
- **Day 3:** Meet your onboarding buddy and set up your development environment.

Your manager will schedule a 30-day check-in. Benefits enrollment must be
completed within **31 days** of your start date, or you must wait for the next
open-enrollment window.
