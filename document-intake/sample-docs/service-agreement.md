# Managed Cleaning Services Agreement

This agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between **Acme Facilities Ltd** ("the Client")
and **BrightSpace Cleaning** ("the Provider").

1. **Term.** 12 months from the signing date, renewing automatically unless either party
   gives 60 days' written notice.
2. **Fees.** 2,400 EUR per month, invoiced on the first working day of each month.
3. **Service levels.** Offices are cleaned five evenings a week; missed visits are credited.
4. **Termination.** Either party may terminate for material breach after a 14-day cure period.
